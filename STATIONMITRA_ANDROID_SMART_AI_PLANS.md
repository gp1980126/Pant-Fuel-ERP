# Commercial Plans — StationMitra Android + Smart AI

These tiers are proposed and configurable:

| Plan | Scope | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Trial | Demo | Limited records, dashboard and reports |
| Starter | 1 pump | Core sales, stock, parties and standard reports |
| Business | 1 pump | Full ERP, Android/PWA, PDF/Excel, backup |
| Professional | 1 pump | Business + Smart AI + WhatsApp + Tally/CA |
| Multi-Pump | Multiple pumps | Central owner dashboard, station isolation, AI insights |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom integrations, users, support and deployment |

Indicative pricing can be configured by the commercial owner; no price is hard-coded in application logic.
